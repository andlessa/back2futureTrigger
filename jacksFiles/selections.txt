B2F generator-level cutflow selections (applied in order)

1. chi1 (PDG 4000023) present with a decay vertex
     d = beta*gamma * VTIMUP        (VTIMUP = invariant c*tau, mm)

2. Fiducial volume (barrel/endcap split at |eta| = 1.05)
     barrel:  3.5 m < R < 7.5 m
     endcap:  7 m < |z| < 13 m

3. Timing: 25 ns <= dt < 50 ns
     dt = (d/c) * (1/beta - 1)      

4. Recoil parton in BC(N)
     status 1, |PDG| <= 6 or 21
     pT > 150 GeV
     |eta| < 2.4
     not a descendant of chi1 (recursive)
     dR > 0.4 from the chi1 direction

5. MET in BC(N) > 200 GeV
     MET = | pT(chi1) + sum pT(stable invisibles not descended from chi1) |

6. RoI proxy >= 3                             [minimal impact at parton level]
     n_RoI = (visible energy of chi1 descendants, excluding chi0) / 4 GeV

Normalisation
     N = sigma * L * eff_kin 
     sigma    = XSECUP from the LHE (branching fractions included)
     eff_kin  = measured fraction surviving 1-6
